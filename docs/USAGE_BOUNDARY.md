# Usage Boundary

The OSIT Formal Lab is public-safe and deliberately narrow.

## Allowed

- Synthetic examples.
- Unit-tested helper metrics.
- Explicit `APPROVE`, `REVIEW` and `BLOCK` gates.
- Documentation that names assumptions, input ranges and falsifiers.
- Public-safe summaries of MEDIOEVO / OSIT operational methodology.

## Not Allowed

- Private books or full canon exports.
- Fragmentos or protected editorial material.
- RPG/TCG mechanics, lore, assets, builds or bridge code.
- Claudio, Wabi, DUAT or GEODIA private runtime internals.
- Raw prompts, local logs, credentials, account state or real datasets.
- Claims of deployment prediction, universal-science proof, AGI or fairness
  guarantees.

## Claim Posture

Every example should remain in one of these lanes:

- `APPROVE`: synthetic, documented and testable.
- `REVIEW`: missing evidence, ambiguous assumptions or broad wording.
- `BLOCK`: private material, secrets, deployment claims or strong scientific
  claims without evidence.
